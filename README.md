## Logging in without wrecking the account

The page is unusually specific here, which helps, because most failures on these accounts come from logging in at the wrong place.

1. Open the App Store and tap the avatar in the upper right corner to open your account page.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Sign Out.
3. Scroll back to the top and enter the purchased account and password directly.

Rules that go with those steps:

- **App Store only.** Do not sign in through Settings or the iCloud section at the top of the settings list. If the page mentions turning off search after logging in, do that.
- **Switch back before you open the game.** Once the download finishes, sign out of the purchased account, sign back into your own Apple ID, then launch the game. The page states that losses caused by getting this order wrong fall on the buyer.
- **Change the password immediately.** Also replace any other verification method you find attached.
- **Don't enable two-factor when prompted.** Declining is the safer move on a shared credential you're about to re-secure yourself.

If you're on a device where you're already signed into iCloud with your own ID, the App Store sign-in swaps only the store account. That's the behavior you want, and it's why the "App Store only" rule exists.
